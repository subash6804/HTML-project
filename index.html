About Wikipedia

Wikipedia is a free, online, multilingual encyclopedia written and maintained by a global community of volunteers known as Wikipedians through open collaboration using wiki software.Founded in 2001, it has become the world's largest and most-read reference work, providing access to the sum of human knowledge through its millions of articles. Hosted by the nonprofit Wikimedia Foundation, Wikipedia is funded primarily by reader donations and is a site that anyone with internet access can edit and improve.

Key Characteristics:

  • Free Access: Wikipedia provides free access to information for everyone on the planet.
  • Collaborative Creation: Articles are collaboratively written and maintained by a community of volunteers using wiki software.
  • Anyone Can Edit: Most Wikipedia pages can be edited by anyone with an internet connection, allowing for continuous improvement and correction.
  • Vast Scope: It is the largest reference work in history, with millions of articles in hundreds of languages.
  • Nonprofit Hosting: The Wikimedia Foundation hosts Wikipedia and is funded by donations.

Taj Mahal:

The Taj Mahal is a white marble mausoleum in Agra, India, built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife, Mumtaz Mahal. Considered a masterpiece of Mughal architecture, the complex includes the main tomb, formal gardens, a mosque, and guest houses. Construction occurred between 1632 and 1648. It is a globally admired symbol of love and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
